Privacy Policy & Data Protection

1. General Information

This website processes personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data in a transparent and secure manner.

2. What Data We Collect

When placing an order, we may collect personal information including your full name, email address, shipping address, and any additional information voluntarily provided through the order form.

3. Purpose of Processing

Your personal data is collected and processed strictly for the purpose of fulfilling your order, including communication, order handling, and delivery. We do not use your data for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

4. Legal Basis

The legal basis for processing your personal data is your consent and the necessity to perform a contract (order fulfillment). By submitting an order, you explicitly agree to this processing.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal data to third parties. Data may only be shared with essential service providers (such as delivery services) strictly for order completion.

6. Data Retention

Your personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose of the order and comply with legal obligations. After this period, your data will be securely deleted.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, request correction or deletion, and object to processing. You may exercise these rights at any time by contacting us.

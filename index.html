Bio

I’m a Slovak singer songwriter and producer creating atmospheric pop with a focus on emotion and storytelling.

I originally come from a background in percussion and composition, and over time I’ve had the chance to perform across Europe and the United States both as a touring drummer and as a solo artist.

A big part of my journey has been busking. I’ve spent years playing on streets in different countries, turning random corners into small stages and meeting people through music in the most direct way possible. That raw connection still shapes how I write and perform.

In 2020, I released my debut single Psycho Love, which reached MTV rotations across Europe and helped me step into the solo space.

I’m currently based in Sweden, continuing to explore my sound and build something honest, blending movement, travel and everyday moments into music that feels real.
